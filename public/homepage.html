Animated illustration: cyan order packets stream continuously from left to right along distributed lanes across a market backdrop. Coral and amber injected messages join a blue lane locally at one of several small junctions spread along the lanes. Depending on the current illustrative event, the changed packets continue rightward, forming a pair, holding and releasing, reordering, changing path or appearance, or slowing and fading locally. Switching events injects a new message into the ongoing stream without resetting it.
